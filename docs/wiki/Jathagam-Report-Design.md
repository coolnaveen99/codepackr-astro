# Jathagam Report Design Standard

## Goal

The 30-page PDF should look like a professionally prepared South Indian horoscope report: clear hierarchy, traditional chart presentation, useful interpretation and consistent pagination.

## Current report audit

The current output has the right broad sections—birth details, Panchangam, D1/D9 charts, planetary positions, Shadbala, Bhava Bala and house/life-area analysis—but many pages use only a small part of the A4 page. The house-phalan pages are especially sparse. The result feels like dashboard cards printed onto pages rather than a composed horoscope book.

## Recommended structure

1. Cover
2. Birth specifications and calculation settings
3. Table of contents
4. Panchangam and birth-star summary
5. Large Rasi D1 chart
6. Large Navamsa D9 chart
7. Divisional charts
8. Full planetary positions table
9. Special lagnas and Upagrahas
10-15. House-wise interpretation
16-19. Life areas
20-23. Dasa/Bhukti timing
24. Shadbala
25. Ashtakavarga
26. Yogas and Doshas
27. Gochara
28. Remedies / Pariharam
29. Summary and glossary
30. Methodology, disclaimer and branding

## Visual rules

- A4 portrait by default; landscape only for genuinely wide tables.
- Consistent header: native name, Jathagam report and section.
- Consistent footer: astro.codepackr.com, calculation school and page number.
- Restrained traditional palette; avoid repeated heavy watermarks.
- Do not shrink Tamil text just to fill a page.
- Avoid large empty areas; let content flow while keeping deliberate chapter breaks.
- Charts must be large enough to read when printed.
- Tables need aligned numbers and consistent columns.
- A Tamil report should not show raw English placeholders such as enemy, friend, Direct or Rupas.
- Tamil and English versions must use the same section order.

## Professional reading flow

Identity -> chart -> planetary evidence -> strength -> house interpretation -> life areas -> timing -> remedies -> summary.

## External format references

Information architecture was reviewed against Tamilcube, Tamilsonline, OlaMatch sample horoscope reports and Rani Astro detailed report structure. These are reference points only; CodePackr must not copy their text or branding.
