## Principles

1. Birth data stays client-side.
2. Calculation school and ayanamsa are transparent.
3. South Indian chart conventions are preserved.
4. Tamil terminology follows normal Tamil Nadu astrology usage.
5. Calculations and interpretation are kept distinct.
6. The full report should read like a professional horoscope book, not printed dashboard cards.
