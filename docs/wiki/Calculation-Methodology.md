# Calculation Methodology

The selected calculation school and ayanamsa must be visible in the report.

## Status labels

Distinguish validated/core calculations from partial or simplified calculations and roadmap items. A simplified calculation must not be presented as fully validated classical astrology.

## Evidence shown with interpretation

Where practical, predictions should expose Lagna/house, planet/sign/degree, nakshatra/pada, house lord, aspects, dignity, Dasa/Bhukti and strength data.

## Regression checks

Calculation changes should be checked against known charts and trusted references for Rasi, Nakshatra/Pada, Lagna, planetary longitudes and Dasa balance.

## Privacy

Birth date, time, place and derived chart data remain in the browser. No astrology calculation requires server-side storage of birth details.
